Festival

Celebrated at the Vadakkunnathan Temple, this spectacular event features 30 caparisoned elephants, mesmerizing percussion, and a grand fireworks display.

India's premier center for performing arts — preserving Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, and other classical traditions since 1930.
