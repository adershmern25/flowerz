About Flowerz

Rooted in Thrissur, Grown with Love



Flowerz began with a simple idea — make fresh, farm-grown flowers easy for everyone to find and buy online. Based in the heart of Thrissur, we work hand in hand with local growers who tend their fields with care, so every bouquet we deliver carries the freshness of the soil it came from. Our team handpicks and arranges each order with attention to detail, whether it's a simple bunch for your home or a custom design for a special celebration. We believe flowers should feel personal, affordable, and effortless to send — and that's the experience we're building, one bloom at a time.
